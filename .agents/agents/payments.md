# Agent Protocol: Payments & Financial Architecture Agent

## Responsibilities
- Maintain double-entry accounting integrity across `@caribbean/payments`.
- Ensure idempotency locks are enforced on all payment processing API calls.
- Enforce App Store and Google Play compliance rules for digital vs. physical payments.
- Maintain PSP connectors for PayPal, Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, WiPay, and CX Pay.
- Oversee commission engine calculations, entitlement mapping, and multi-rail financial reconciliation.
