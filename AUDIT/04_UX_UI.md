# 04_UX_UI.md — UX/UI & Cultural Authenticity Audit

## 1. Cultural Authenticity vs. Stereotype Guardrails
- **Color Palette:** Obsidian Dark (`#090D16`), Azure Accent (`#0284C7`), Gold/Amber Accent (`#F59E0B`), Emerald Accent (`#059669`).
- **No Tourist Clichés:** No cheesy turquoise wave gradients, flag spamming, or palm tree graphics.
- **Rhythm & Emotion:** High visual contrast, clean typography, rich video/audio players, and celebratory virtual gift animations.

## 2. Interaction Design
- **Empty States Transformed:** Zero dead "Your feed is quiet" screens. Curated cultural moments and Caribbean Now live feeds populate discovery feeds.
- **Buttons & Interactive States:** All action buttons provide optimistic feedback, loading spinners, and clear state confirmations.
