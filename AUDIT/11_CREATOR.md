# 11_CREATOR.md — Creator Economy & Studio Hub

## 1. Monetization Engine
- **Monetization Architecture:** Creators establish tiered subscriptions (e.g. Basic, Plus, Pro, Custom), direct livestream tipping/gifting, and digital product sales with transparent platform service fees.
- **Payout Evaluation:** Minimum threshold of $50.00 minor (5000) verified with KYC status and chargeback reserve hold logic.
