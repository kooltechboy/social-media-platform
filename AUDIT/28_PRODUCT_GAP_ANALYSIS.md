# 28 — PRODUCT STRATEGY & USER JOURNEY GAP ANALYSIS

**Domain:** Core User Journeys, Creator Funnels & Feature Completeness  
**Auditor:** Chief Product Officer & Product Director  
**Status:** Strategic Product Assessment

---

## 1. Persona Journey Analysis

### 1. The Diaspora Member (Toronto / Brooklyn / Miami / London)
- **Goal:** Stay connected with home island news, carnival band launches, music premieres, and authentic diaspora businesses.
- **Experience:** Excellent. Feed filtering by country/city and Ask Caribbean AI search deliver hyper-relevant cultural content unavailable on Instagram or TikTok.

### 2. The Caribbean Creator (Musician, Podcaster, Visual Artist)
- **Goal:** Monetize global diaspora audience directly in USD/CAD/GBP and receive seamless payouts without US bank account barriers.
- **Experience:** Payments multi-currency ledger and tiered subscriptions provide direct creator-to-fan monetization with transparent platform service fees.

### 3. The Island Business (Restaurant, Festival Organizer, Artisan)
- **Goal:** Reach both local customers and returning diaspora visitors with verified business profiles and event ticketing.
- **Experience:** Direct marketplace storefronts with low 8% commission and built-in dispute resolution.
