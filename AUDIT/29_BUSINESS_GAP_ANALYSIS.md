## 1. Multi-Sided Monetization Model

TUKUBI does not depend solely on advertising arbitrage. It deploys a diversified, multi-engine revenue strategy:

```
1. Creator Subscriptions: 15% Platform Take Rate
2. Live Stream Virtual Gifts: 20-30% Margin
3. Marketplace & Product Sales: 8% Take Rate
4. Event Ticketing: $1.00 + 2.5% per ticket
5. Business Verified Profiles & Sponsored Placements: Monthly SaaS Tier ($29 - $199/mo)
6. SpotPay Cross-Border FX & Remittance Orchestration: 1.5 - 2.5% FX Spread
```
